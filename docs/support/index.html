TH Hunter · Help center

How can we help?

Questions, bug reports, or suggestions for TH Hunter? Contact us directly, or check the answers to common questions below.

Contact support

For help with the app, missing car information, or purchase issues, email us at developerSims@icloud.com.

Email support

To help us investigate, include your device model, Android or iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful, but please don't send passwords or payment details.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my collection information stored?

Your found-car selections and collection information are stored locally on your device. TH Hunter does not require an account to track your collection. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove that information.

Are my collection photos uploaded?

No. Photos you choose for your collection remain on your device and are not uploaded to TH Hunter's backend. Be aware that your device's own photo backup settings may apply.

Why isn't a car or year appearing?

TH Hunter's catalog is updated as reference information becomes available. If a car is missing or a listing seems incorrect, email support with the car name, year, and whether it is a Treasure Hunt (TH) or Super Treasure Hunt (STH).

How do I change advertising privacy choices?

Open TH Hunter's About screen and select Privacy Choices where available. You can also review advertising preferences through your device or Google account settings.

What if my optional support purchase has an issue?

Please contact support and describe what happened, including the app version and platform. Purchases are processed by the relevant app store. Do not email payment card numbers or other sensitive payment information.

Can I transfer my collection to a new phone?

TH Hunter does not currently provide account-based collection syncing. Because collection data is stored locally, do not assume it will transfer automatically to another device.
