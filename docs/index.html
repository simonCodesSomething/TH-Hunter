TH HUNTER

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 9, 2026

TH Hunter helps users browse Hot Wheels Treasure Hunt and Super Treasure Hunt cars and track their personal collections. This policy explains how information is stored, accessed, and processed when you use the app.

Collection information

Your collected-car selections, collection progress, and collection photos are stored locally on your device. TH Hunter does not require an account or upload your personal collection to our backend. Removing the app may delete this locally stored information.

Photos

You may choose photos from your device's photo library to associate with your collection. These photos are used locally within the app and are not uploaded to our servers. Photo access is requested only when needed for this feature.

Catalog and backend services

TH Hunter uses Supabase to retrieve reference information, including car listings and catalog updates. The app does not send your personal collection or selected photos to Supabase. Network requests may involve technical information such as IP addresses and request metadata processed by the service provider.

Advertising

TH Hunter displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect, use, and share information such as advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device information, app interactions, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, provide analytics, and prevent fraud or invalid advertising activity. IP addresses may also be used to estimate a device's approximate geographic location.

Depending on your location, applicable laws, device settings, and available privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. Limiting ad personalization does not necessarily prevent all information collection or processing.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising privacy choices

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, Google may provide additional choices regarding the use of your information for advertising.

Where available, you can access these controls through the Privacy choices option in TH Hunter's About screen. You may also manage certain advertising preferences through your device and Google account settings.

Optional support purchases

TH Hunter offers an optional support purchase processed through Google Play Billing. This contribution supports app development and does not unlock additional features or remove advertisements. You can choose to contribute more than once.

Google Play processes payment and transaction information according to its own policies. TH Hunter receives purchase status and transaction information needed to complete the purchase. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card details.

Accounts and personal information

TH Hunter does not require account creation, and we do not operate a user account database for the app. Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play may process information as described in this policy.

Data sharing

TH Hunter does not sell your personal collection information or upload your collection photos to third parties.

Advertising, billing, and backend providers may process technical or transaction information as necessary to provide their respective services, subject to their privacy policies and applicable law.

Data security

TH Hunter uses encrypted HTTPS connections to communicate with its backend and integrated services.

Your collection information and personal collection photos are stored locally on your device. Their protection also depends on your device's security settings and operating system.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention and deletion

Collection information and photos stored locally remain on your device until you remove them through available app controls, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup settings.

Data processed by Google and other service providers may be retained according to their respective policies. For questions about information associated with TH Hunter, you may contact us using the email below.

Children's privacy

TH Hunter is intended for collectors aged 18 and older and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children. Third-party advertising and purchase services process information according to their applicable policies.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the email address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in TH Hunter, its services, or applicable requirements. Updated versions will include a revised date and will be made available through the app and on this page.
